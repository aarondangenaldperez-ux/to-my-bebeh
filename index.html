A Little Thanks Goes a Long Way

You probably don't realize how much I appreciate the little things we do together. They're just ordinary moments, but honestly, they're some of my favorite things about us. It's always the simple things with you that I end up remembering the most.

Make It Through the Rain

As time goes by, I've realized that I've started finding a lot of encouragement in you. You inspire me in ways you probably don't even notice. Ikaw ang Snoopy ng buhay ko. Just like how Snoopy is always there to listen to Charlie Brown, you're always there to make things feel a little lighter whenever I'm overwhelmed.

When in Doubt, Dance!

I'm not really the jolly type, and I don't usually show that side of myself. But whenever I'm with you, I somehow become more playful and carefree. I get to be silly without thinking too much about it, just like Snoopy doing his happy dance. I guess you're the person I'm most comfortable being this way with.

Share the Journey

I love how we're not just partners, but friends too. Having you beside me makes this whole journey through young adulthood a little easier and more bearable. Just like Snoopy staying beside Charlie Brown, it's comforting knowing I have someone to share all these experiences with. I'm really glad that I get to experience this part of my life with my best friend and my partner.
